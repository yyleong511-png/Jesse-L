01

Podcast — Two Chatters

People from completely different walks of life still carry their own stories worth telling — that idea sits behind Two Chatters. It actually started alone: talking out loud to an empty room, the way some people think best out loud rather than in silence. Recording it on a phone turned that habit into something worth keeping, and finding a like-minded friend to sit across the mic turned it into a proper, ongoing project — still, at its core, just a fun thing to make together.
